WoWS Toolbox 5.0.84

- Add independent hull, main-battery and torpedo research selectors for PC and Korabli ships. All three default to the fully upgraded configuration.
- Rebuild AA, secondaries and other mounted equipment from the chosen hull; resolve weapon upgrades through that hull's compatible components.
- Retain compatible weapon research choices on hull changes; reset incompatible choices to the final available upgrade.
- Preserve selections in saved queues, output metadata, cache keys and request identities. Reject stale/foreign module IDs instead of silently falling back.
- Use the same module-aware native exporter for visible geometry and exact armor, including ships with separate hull model directories.
- Keep Legends and Blitz on their existing fixed configurations.

Usage: refresh the ship catalog, add/select a queued ship, then click Select modules below the output controls. Choose A/B cards in three research columns; Fully upgrade all restores defaults.
Validation: affected backend regression tests, native library tests, actual WPF selectors and queue checks; Charles Martel stock/researched guns on hull A and researched guns on hull B; textured OBJ/MTL/PNG and armor exports for Charles Martel and Icarus. Installed PC data confirmed 16 AA mounts on Charles Martel hull A and 24 on hull B. Icarus researched torpedoes produced two five-tube launchers.

- 부품 조합을 대기열 저장, 캐시, 출력 폴더와 추출 기록에 반영하고 모델과 장갑에도 같은 조합을 적용해요.
